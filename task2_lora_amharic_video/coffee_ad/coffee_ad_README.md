# Ethiopian coffee advertisement (pretrained Wan2.1-1.3B on a free Colab T4)

A 15-second vertical (9:16) advertisement made from four AI-generated scenes, with exact Amharic titles and an Amharic voice-over added in post-production.
**No coffee LoRA was trained for this video.** The scenes come from pretrained Wan2.1-T2V-1.3B; the only LoRA in this repository (Ge'ez-word clips) was not used here.

Final video: `task2_lora_amharic_video/outputs/coffee_ad/ethiopian_coffee_ad.mp4` (silent version: `ad_silent.mp4`)
Files: `gen_scenes.py` (GPU stage), `assemble.py` (titles and editing), `voiceover.py` (narration and final mix).

## 1. Why the work is split into three stages

| Design decision | Reason |
|---|---|
| Amharic text is **not** generated by the video model | In the Task 2 comparison, base Wan produced fragmented, Latin-looking shapes instead of Ge'ez script, and the LoRA only reached "Ge'ez-style" glyphs, not exact words. Titles are therefore drawn programmatically with a real Ethiopic font, which guarantees correct spelling. |
| Four short clips, not one 15 s generation | Wan 1.3B on a 15 GB T4 handles short, low-resolution clips only. |
| Voice-over added separately | Wan produces silent video. |
| GPU used only for stage 1 | Editing and narration run on CPU, so only about 4 generations need the quota-limited GPU. |

## 2. Experimental flow

```
4 text prompts --> [gen_scenes.py, T4 GPU] --> scene1..4.mp4 (288x512, 33 frames, 16 fps)
--> [assemble.py, CPU] frame blending, dissolves, upscale to 720x1280, Amharic titles --> ad_silent.mp4 (about 15 s)
--> [voiceover.py] Amharic TTS, timed lines, mux --> ethiopian_coffee_ad.mp4
```

1. **Stage 1, scene generation** (Colab, T4 GPU). Prompts are encoded once with Wan's multilingual umt5 text encoder, the 11 GB encoder is then freed, and the pipeline runs with the transformer in fp16 and the VAE in fp32 with tiling. Each scene is one generation with classifier-free guidance.
2. **Selection.** Scenes were reviewed visually. Scene 3 was regenerated once with another seed and that version was kept.
3. **Stage 2, assembly** (CPU). Each 33-frame clip is expanded to 65 frames by inserting a blended frame between neighbours (smoother slow motion), clips are joined with 6-frame cross-dissolves, frames are upscaled with Lanczos and the title is drawn on the lower third.
4. **Stage 3, narration** (CPU or GPU). Three Amharic lines are synthesised and placed at fixed times, then muxed with the video.
